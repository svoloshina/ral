Participate in Research

Healthy volunteers and individuals with movement impairments are welcome to participate in our studies.

Our lab conducts studies on human movement, balance, and walking. We are always recruiting participants for our ongoing research projects.

If you are interested in participating, please contact us at the email below. We will follow up with details about eligibility and study procedures.
