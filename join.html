Join the Lab

We welcome motivated students and researchers at all levels.

We are always looking for passionate, curious people to join our team. If you are interested in human movement, wearable robotics, rehabilitation, or biomechanics, we'd love to hear from you.

Graduate students

We recruit PhD and MS students through the MAE and BME graduate programs at UCI. Prospective students should apply through the relevant program and mention the lab in their application.

Contact Prof. Voloshina

Undergraduate students

UCI undergraduates from any engineering or science background are encouraged to reach out. Research opportunities are available for course credit or as part of the UROP program.

Contact Prof. Voloshina

Postdoctoral researchers

Prospective postdocs with a background in biomechanics, robotics, neuroscience, or rehabilitation engineering are encouraged to contact Prof. Voloshina directly with a CV and brief statement of interest.

Contact Prof. Voloshina
