Join us!

We welcome motivated students and researchers at all levels.

Any open positions will be listed here. Please see below for specific instructions on how to apply.

Postdoctoral Fellows

If you are interested in postdoctoral positions in the lab, please contact Dr. Voloshina directly.

PhD Students

No opportunities are currently available. If you are interested in applying to the UCI MAE graduate program, you can find more information on the MAE graduate program page ↗.

MS Students

We are currently looking for Master’s students to start in the 2026–2027 academic year. If you are interested in a research position, please contact Dr. Voloshina. In the email, please include a PDF of your CV, a PDF of your undergraduate and graduate transcripts, and a brief description of your research interests and career goals.

Undergraduate Students

Limited undergraduate research opportunities are available each quarter, generally through research credits such as MAE199. Please contact Dr. Voloshina to inquire about open positions.

High School Students
